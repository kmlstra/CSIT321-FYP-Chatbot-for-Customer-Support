Step 3: Field Mapping

Map your database fields to our system:

  • Vehicle Brand Field: brand or make
  • Vehicle Model Field: model or vehicle_name
  • Price Field: price or selling_price
  • Year Field: year or model_year
  • Availability Field: status or availability
  • Table/Collection Name: vehicles or inventory

Step 4: Test & Sync

1. Click "🔍 Test Connection" to verify connectivity

2. If successful, click "💾 Save Configuration"

3. Click "🔄 Sync Data" to pull your inventory

4. Check the "🚗 Inventory" tab to see your synced vehicles
